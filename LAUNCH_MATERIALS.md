## Sample Responses to Common Questions

**Q: Is it really free?**
A: Yes! Hamboi MindCare is 100% free. No hidden costs, no subscriptions, no payments ever.

**Q: Is my information private?**
A: Completely! The platform doesn't require sign-up. Your journal entries and mood data stay on your device. The AI chat is anonymous.

**Q: Do I need to download an app?**
A: No! Just visit hamboimindcare.site on any phone or computer. It works right in your browser.

**Q: Is this for Nigerians only?**
A: It's designed with Nigerian teens in mind, but anyone can use it!

**Q: What if I'm in crisis?**
A: The platform has crisis resources with direct numbers to professional help. Please reach out to trained counselors if you're in immediate danger.

## Tips for Maximum Reach

1. **Post at peak times:** 7-9 AM, 12-2 PM, 7-9 PM (when students are active)
2. **Use hashtags:** Mix popular and niche hashtags
3. **Tag relevant accounts:** Nigerian youth influencers, mental health advocates
4. **Engage immediately:** Reply to every comment in first 2 hours
5. **Ask for shares:** "Tag 3 friends who should see this"
6. **Create urgency:** "Launch day - check it out now!"
7. **Use testimonials:** After a few days, share positive feedback
8. **Post consistently:** Daily updates for first week
